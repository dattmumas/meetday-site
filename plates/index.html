How it picks plates

Few plates, big plates inside

  • The fewest plates per sideOf all the ways to make the load, the calculator picks the one with the fewest plates. Big plates sit inside.
  • Only loads your rack can makeYour lightest pair of plates sets the smallest jump. With 2.5 lb plates, loads go up 5 lb at a time.
  • The closest loadsWhen a target is not possible, it shows the closest loads below and above it.
  • Warm-ups in platesSteps at about 50, 70 and 90 percent, each at a load that needs few plates. Each step keeps the big plates on and changes the rest.

In the app, Today > Settings > Your gym holds your plates and bars. Every load you see is drawn with them, and the rulebook only declares loads your gym can make.

The warm-up screen walks you up the ladder, one step at a time. Warm-ups are not judged.

Warm-ups step up in plates

Before each work set, Meet Day plans the warm-ups to it. Each step names the plates to take off and put on: 25 off, 45 on.

Each lift also shows its next plate milestone: one plate a side, then two. Logging, warm-ups and your gym are free.
