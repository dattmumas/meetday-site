Privacy

Effective September 24, 2026

Meet Day is an iPhone app for logging strength training, operated by Matthew Dumas ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the control you have over it. Contact: support@lnkd.world.

Apple processes the payment for Meet Day Pro. We never receive your payment card or your Apple Account details.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or browsing history. The app has no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase, Inc., on Amazon Web Services infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Database rules restrict every record to its owner's account. The session in progress is also kept on your iPhone until you finish or discard it. The widgets and the rest clock on the Lock Screen read a copy of your next session that stays on your iPhone.

Who can see it

Only you. We share data only with service providers that run the app for us, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and server functions
  • Resend: sends confirmation and password reset codes to your email address
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, if you use it, and payments for Meet Day Pro through the App Store
  • Anthropic: answers the trainer's questions with Claude, only after you allow it (see The AI trainer)

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

The AI trainer

With Meet Day Pro on iOS 27, you can ask the trainer about your training. To answer, the trainer reads your training data in the app: sessions, workouts, loads, reps, RPE, your block and your gym. It proposes changes, and nothing changes until you tap Apply.

  • Apple Intelligence: when your iPhone supports it, the trainer answers with Apple's model on Private Cloud Compute. Apple processes the request to answer it and does not store it.
  • Claude by Anthropic: when Apple's model is not available, the trainer can answer with Claude, and only after you allow it in the app. Your question and the training data the trainer needs go to Anthropic through our server. Your name, email and account details are not sent. Anthropic uses the data to answer and does not train its models on it, under its commercial terms. You can take the permission back in Today > Settings > AI trainer.

The chat stays on your iPhone, and deleting your account deletes it. Our server does not store your questions or the answers; it counts messages and tokens for the monthly allowance.

Connected assistants and API keys

You can connect an AI assistant you already use, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, by signing in with Meet Day on our sign-in page and tapping Allow. The assistant then reads and changes your training data through Meet Day's tools, as you. It cannot see your password, make API keys or delete your account. The data it receives is governed by that assistant's own privacy policy. See and revoke connections in Today > Settings > Connected assistants.

For tools on your own computer, such as Claude Code, you can create personal API keys in Today > Settings > Advanced. Anyone holding a key can read and change your training data, so share keys only with tools you trust. You can revoke a key at any time. We store only a one-way hash of each key.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete your account and all of its data, open Today > Settings > Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and it also ends every connected assistant, removes your API keys and trainer usage records, and deletes the trainer's chat on that iPhone. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke the app's Apple sign-in tokens. Deleting your account does not cancel a Meet Day Pro subscription: Apple bills it until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions). Copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a limited period before they are overwritten. You can also email us to request deletion or a copy of your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Most of these you can do in the app. For anything else, email support@lnkd.world and we will respond within 30 days.

Health information

Meet Day is a training log. It does not give medical advice and is not a medical device, and the trainer gives no diagnosis or treatment. We use your training and body data only to show it to you and, when you ask, for the trainer's answers.

Children

Meet Day is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app before the change applies.
