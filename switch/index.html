Coming from Hevy · free

Bring your Hevy history

Import your Hevy sessions into Meet Day. You check every exercise before the import. Nothing changes in Hevy.

Coming soon to the App Store

The import is free, with or without Meet Day Pro.

Two ways in

Export a file, or use a key

Open Import from Hevy in Today > Settings, or on the Workouts tab. Then pick one way.

CSV export · sessions

  1. In Hevy, open Profile > Settings > Export & Import Data > Export Data > Export Workouts.

  2. Save the file to Files.

  3. In Meet Day, tap Choose the CSV file. Then choose the file you saved.

API key · Hevy Pro · sessions and workouts

  1. Get your API key at hevy.com/settings?developer. The API key is a Hevy Pro feature.

  2. In Meet Day, paste the key and tap Fetch from Hevy.

The key also brings your routines. They become workouts, in their folders. Meet Day uses the key for this import only and does not store it.

Before the import

Check every match

Meet Day matches each Hevy exercise to its library of 628 exercises. The review screen shows every match before anything is saved.

Examples of how Hevy names match the Meet Day library
Name in HevyIn Meet Day
Squat (Barbell)As Back Squat (Barbell)
Bent Over Row (Barbell)As Bent-Over Row (Barbell)
Incline Dumbbell PressAs Incline Bench Press (Dumbbell)
Butterfly (Pec Deck)As Pec Deck (Machine)

Tap an exercise to change what it becomes. An exercise with no match comes in as a custom exercise with its Hevy name.

Then tap Import. Keep the app open until it finishes. If it stops, run it again. Sessions that are already here are skipped.

Imported sessions are not judged, and they do not move your workouts on. Your history, records and charts are ready at once.

After the import

Your next session gets judged

Start a session from any workout. With Meet Day Pro, three lamps judge each declared set. Then the rulebook sets your next loads.

Your records and charts come from the sessions you import. Each lift shows its best set and its estimated max.

Questions

Short answers

Does the import change my Hevy account?

No. The import only reads your data. Nothing changes in Hevy.

Can I import again later?

Yes. Importing again skips what is already here. A session from the CSV file and the same session from the API key count as one.

Does Meet Day keep my Hevy key?

No. The key is used for this import only and is not stored.

What does the import cost?

Nothing. The import is free. Logging and history are free too.
