Step by step

Set it up in December

Set up the block before the new year. Today shows the first session on Monday, January 4.

  1. Open Programs, from the Workouts tab, Today, Settings or the Trainer tab. Programs that fit About you come first.

  2. Pick a program and tap Start this program.

  3. Set the first day to Monday, January 4. Name the block, then tap Start. With Meet Day Pro, the program becomes a block.

  4. Check your training days in Edit block: a workout or Rest for each day, Monday first. The program's week comes with it.

  5. Lift. Your first session sets each load. After that, every good session moves the lift up.

Programs have sets, rep ranges and rest, but no loads. The first session sets each load from your heaviest work set, with the call Set.

Miss a day, and the session moves to the next free day of that week. Accept the move, or pick another day.

On iOS 27, the AI trainer (Pro) can move the days or swap a lift for you. Ask it from the Trainer tab.

Without Pro, a program starts as a folder of workouts. There is no block and no automatic progression.

The rulebook Pro

Good sessions move the bar up

After each session, the rulebook writes the card with your next loads. It uses only loads your plates can make.

The five rules and the call each one writes
1A good lift: all reps, full load, RPE 8 or less.
2Every set a good lift: the next card goes up.A barbell goes up 5 lb or 2.5 kg. In a rep range, a rep comes first.+5Reps +1
3Any red light: same load next time.Hold
4Two holds in a row: reset 10% and build back.Reset
5Deload week: lighter bar, fewer sets.Nothing moves.Deload

Questions

Short answers

Do I need Meet Day Pro?

For the block and the rulebook, yes. Pro Yearly starts with a one-week free trial for new subscribers. Without Pro, you can still log every session.

Can I start on another day?

Yes. The first day is yours to pick. A block counts its weeks from that day.

Which program fits me?

Fill in About you. It is free. Programs that fit your goal, your level, your days and your time come first.

What if my gym has no small plates?

The rulebook picks the next load your plates can make. Tell Meet Day your plates in Today > Settings > Your gym.
