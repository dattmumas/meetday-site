The lamps Pro

A white lamp is a pass

Each set with a declared load or reps gets three lamps. One red lamp makes it a no lift, and the verdict names the reason.

  • Good liftAll reps, full load, RPE 8 or less.
  • No lift · repsShort of the declared reps.
  • No lift · loadUnder the declared load.
  • No lift · effortRPE 9 or 10.
  • Not judgedWarm-ups, and sets with no declared load.

No RPE counts as a pass. The set screen logs a pass by default. Tap Fail to record RPE 9.

For an assisted lift, less help is the harder lift. The load lamp is white at the declared help or less.

The rulebook Pro

Five rules write the card

When you save a session of a workout, the rulebook moves each lift. The card shows every change with its call.

The five rules and the call each one writes
1A good lift: all reps, full load, RPE 8 or less.
2Every set a good lift: the next card goes up.A barbell goes up 5 lb or 2.5 kg.+5
3Any red light: same load next time.Hold
4Two holds in a row: reset 10% and build back.Reset
5Deload week: lighter bar, fewer sets.Nothing moves.Deload

In a rep range, reps come first. Every set good adds a rep, with the call Reps +1. At the top of the range, the load goes up a step and the reps start again at the bottom.

Other calls on the card

  • SameThe load does not move.
  • SetThe first session of a lift with no declared load sets it, from your heaviest work set.
  • YouYou changed the line on the card.
  • NewOn Today: a lift with no session yet.

Accept the card, or change a line first. Change attempt sets another load for one lift.

When it runs

Only for a new session

The rulebook runs once, when you save a new session of a workout. Undo on the card puts the workout back. So does deleting the session.

Nothing moves for

  • An imported session.
  • A session older than the newest one of that workout.
  • A workout you changed during the session.
  • An account without Meet Day Pro.

Loads your gym can make

A new load is always one your plates can make. When the next step needs a plate you do not have, the rulebook picks the next load you can build. When no load is within reach, the lift holds.
