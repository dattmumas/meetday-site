What it does Pro · iOS 27

Every change is a proposal

The trainer can read your whole log. To change it, the trainer makes a proposal. Nothing changes until you tap Apply.

  • Explains the card and your lightsAsk why a lift held. The trainer reads the scoresheet and tells you.
  • Moves a missed sessionIt proposes a free day that week for the session you missed.
  • Builds your next blockIt starts from one of the 21 programs and fits it to About you and your gym.
  • Changes a workoutTraveling, or at another gym? Ask for a swap. It proposes the new workout.
  • Writes the callAfter each judged session, it writes one or two plain sentences on the card.

Open the trainer from the Trainer tab, or from the round button on Today, Workouts, Block and History.

The trainer keeps to your training and the app. It gives no medical advice. For pain or an injury, it tells you to stop that movement and see a doctor or a physical therapist.

On iOS 26 and earlier, the Trainer tab says that the AI trainer needs iOS 27. The rulebook still writes the card after each session.

Who answers Pro · iOS 27

Who answers your question

The trainer uses the first of these that can answer.

  1. Your own Claude key. Add an Anthropic API key in Today > Settings > AI trainer. Claude Sonnet 5 then answers every message on your Anthropic account. Anthropic bills you, and Meet Day sets no monthly limit.

  2. Apple's model on Private Cloud Compute. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's model answers. Apple uses your data for the answer and does not store it.

  3. Claude by Anthropic, through Meet Day. Claude answers after you allow it. Meet Day Pro includes 60 Claude messages a month. The count starts again on the first of the month.

Before a question goes to Anthropic for the first time, a sheet names Anthropic and lists what is sent. Allow sends the question. Not now sends nothing.

Today > Settings > AI trainer shows the messages left this month. You can take the permission back there, or remove your key.

The chat stays on your iPhone. Deleting your account deletes it.

Connected assistants Pro

Connect Claude by signing in

An assistant you already use, such as Claude, can read your log and plan your workouts. You connect it by signing in. There is no key to copy.

Connect Claude

  1. In Meet Day, open Today > Settings > Connect an assistant. Copy Meet Day's address.

  2. In Claude, open Customize > Connectors. Tap + and choose Add custom connector.

  3. Paste the address. Then sign in with your Meet Day email and a code or your password.

  4. Tap Allow.

It works on every Claude plan. The free plan allows one custom connector.

Signed up with Apple? Set a password in Connect an assistant first. Then sign in with the email the app shows and that password.

What an assistant can do

The assistant acts as you in Meet Day through its tools. It can read your log. It can save a workout or a block, and it can log a session for you. With Meet Day Pro, a session it logs moves the workout on, as one you log in the app.

What it cannot do

It cannot see your password. It cannot manage API keys or delete your account. What it receives follows the assistant's own privacy terms.

Disconnect

Open Today > Settings > Connected assistants and revoke the assistant. Its access ends at once.

  • GeminiAdd a custom app with Meet Day's address, then sign in. Gemini offers custom apps in the US, in English.
  • ChatGPTChatGPT adds apps from its app directory. Meet Day is not listed yet.
  • Claude Code and scriptsUse an API key from Today > Settings > Advanced > API Keys. Making a key is part of Meet Day Pro.

Connected assistants are part of Meet Day Pro. Without Pro, an assistant can sign in but cannot read or log anything.
