Questions

I did not get a confirmation or reset code.

Check your spam folder, then tap Resend code. Codes come from no-reply@lnkd.world and expire after one hour.

Why was my set a no lift?

With Meet Day Pro, each set with a declared load gets three lights. REPS is white when you did every declared rep. LOAD is white when the weight was at least the declared load (for assisted lifts, at most). EFFORT is white at RPE 8 or less. One red light makes the set a no lift. Warm-ups are not judged.

A set did not save.

Only logged sets are saved when you finish a session. Open the session from History, tap the menu, then Edit Workout to add sets.

How do I switch between kg and lb?

Today > Settings > Weight. Your data is stored in kg and converts on screen. Set the plates and bars you have in Today > Settings > Your gym.

What can the trainer do?

In the Trainer tab, tap Ask the trainer. It explains the card and your lights, moves a missed session, adjusts a workout for travel or a sore shoulder, and builds your next block. Each change comes as a proposal: nothing changes until you tap Apply. After each judged session it also writes the call for the next one. The trainer is part of Meet Day Pro and needs iOS 27. It gives no medical advice.

Which AI answers, and what does it cost?

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's model on Private Cloud Compute answers at no extra cost. Otherwise the trainer can use Claude by Anthropic once you allow it: Meet Day Pro includes 60 Claude messages a month, and the count resets on the first of the month. Settings > AI trainer shows what is left and lets you turn Claude off.

How do I connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini?

Today > Settings > Connect an assistant shows Meet Day's address. In Claude, open Customize > Connectors, add a custom connector with that address, sign in with your Meet Day email and a code or your password, and tap Allow. Gemini adds it as a custom app in the US. ChatGPT lists apps in its directory, where Meet Day is not yet. Connected assistants need Meet Day Pro. See or revoke them in Settings > Connected assistants.

I signed up with Apple. How do I sign in on the connect page?

Open Settings > Connect an assistant, set a password there, then sign in on the page with the email shown in the app and that password.

Can I still use an API key?

Yes, for tools on your computer such as Claude Code: Today > Settings > Advanced > API Keys (part of Meet Day Pro). Revoke a key there at any time.

What does Meet Day Pro add, and what does it cost?

Logging, workouts, history and the Hevy import are free. Pro adds the lights on every declared set, the Trainer's card, blocks with a weekly schedule, the AI trainer (iOS 27), and connecting your own AI assistant. Pro Monthly is $4.99 a month. Pro Yearly is $39.99 a year and starts with a one-week free trial for new subscribers. Prices in other countries follow the App Store.

How do I cancel or change Meet Day Pro?

Apple bills the subscription. Open Today > Settings > Meet Day Pro > Manage subscription, or on your iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Meet Day. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed, but the app does not show Pro.

Sign in to the Meet Day account you subscribed with, then tap Restore purchases in Today > Settings > Meet Day Pro. Restore also moves a subscription to the account you are signed in to.

How do I delete my account?

Today > Settings > Delete Account. This removes your account and all training data. It does not cancel a Meet Day Pro subscription; cancel that first in Manage subscription.
