Questions

I did not get a confirmation or reset code.

Check your spam folder, then tap Resend code. Codes come from no-reply@lnkd.world and expire after one hour.

Why was my set a no lift?

Each set with a declared load gets three lights. REPS is white when you did every declared rep. LOAD is white when the weight was at least the declared load (for assisted lifts, at most). EFFORT is white at RPE 8 or less. One red light makes the set a no lift. Warm-ups are not judged.

A set did not save.

Only logged sets are saved when you finish a session. Open the session from History, tap the menu, then Edit Workout to add sets.

How do I switch between kg and lb?

Today > Settings > Weight. Your data is stored in kg and converts on screen. Set the plates and bars you have in Today > Settings > Your gym.

How do I connect an AI assistant?

Today > Settings > API Keys. Create a key and give it to your assistant's Meet Day connector. Revoke the key there at any time.

How do I delete my account?

Today > Settings > Delete Account. This removes your account and all training data.
