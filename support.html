Questions

I did not get a confirmation or reset code.

Check your spam folder, then tap Resend code. Codes come from no-reply@lnkd.world and expire after one hour.

Why was my set a no lift?

With Meet Day Pro, each set with a declared load gets three lights. REPS is white when you did every declared rep. LOAD is white when the weight was at least the declared load (for assisted lifts, at most). EFFORT is white at RPE 8 or less. One red light makes the set a no lift. Warm-ups are not judged.

A set did not save.

Only logged sets are saved when you finish a session. Open the session from History, tap the menu, then Edit Workout to add sets.

How do I switch between kg and lb?

Today > Settings > Weight. Your data is stored in kg and converts on screen. Set the plates and bars you have in Today > Settings > Your gym.

How do I connect an AI assistant?

Today > Settings > API Keys (part of Meet Day Pro). Create a key and give it to your assistant's Meet Day connector. Revoke the key there at any time.

What does Meet Day Pro add, and what does it cost?

Logging, workouts, history and the Hevy import are free. Pro adds the lights on every declared set, the Trainer's card, blocks with a weekly schedule, and API keys for your AI assistant. Pro Monthly is $4.99 a month. Pro Yearly is $39.99 a year and starts with a one-week free trial for new subscribers. Prices in other countries follow the App Store.

How do I cancel or change Meet Day Pro?

Apple bills the subscription. Open Today > Settings > Meet Day Pro > Manage subscription, or on your iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Meet Day. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed, but the app does not show Pro.

Sign in to the Meet Day account you subscribed with, then tap Restore purchases in Today > Settings > Meet Day Pro. Restore also moves a subscription to the account you are signed in to.

How do I delete my account?

Today > Settings > Delete Account. This removes your account and all training data. It does not cancel a Meet Day Pro subscription; cancel that first in Manage subscription.
